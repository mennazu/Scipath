🐾 Zoology

📌 About the Specialization

Zoology is the study of animals, their structure, physiology, behavior, evolution, classification, and interactions with the environment. The specialization combines laboratory and field studies and provides knowledge applicable to research, wildlife, environmental, and biological fields.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Strong interest in Biology and animals
  • Basic knowledge of Chemistry and Genetics
  • Observation and laboratory skills
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Interest in animal behavior and ecology
  • Willingness to do fieldwork

📚 Courses you will study

  • Invertebrate Zoology
  • Vertebrate Zoology
  • Cell Biology & Genetics
  • Animal Physiology
  • Animal Ecology
  • Entomology
  • Experimental Zoology
  • Histology
  • Comparative Anatomy
  • Animal Behavior
  • Immunology
  • Molecular Biology
