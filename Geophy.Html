🌋 Geophysics

📌 About the Specialization

Geophysics applies physics and mathematics to study the Earth's structure, properties, and processes. It focuses on using geophysical methods to explore underground resources, groundwater, minerals, and the Earth's subsurface.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Strong interest in Physics and Mathematics
  • Interest in Earth sciences and exploration
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work with scientific instruments and data
  • Willingness to do fieldwork

📚 Courses you will study

  • Principles of Geophysics
  • Seismology
  • Magnetic Methods
  • Seismic Methods
  • Electrical Methods
  • Electromagnetic Methods
  • Well Logging
  • Geophysical Surveys
  • Geophysical Data Processing
  • Geophysical Instrumentation
  • Exploration Geophysics
  • Hydrogeophysics
