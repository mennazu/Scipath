💧🐟 Aquaculture

📌 About the Specialization

Aquaculture is a field that focuses on the breeding, rearing, and production of aquatic organisms such as fish, crustaceans, and mollusks. It covers areas such as nutrition, reproduction, water quality, disease management, and sustainable aquaculture practices.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Biology and aquatic life
  • Basic knowledge of Chemistry
  • Good observation skills
  • Problem-solving skills
  • nterest in practical and field work

📚 Courses you will study

  • Fish Biology
  • Fish Physiology
  • Fish Behavior, Reproduction and Nutrition
  • Fish Farming and Production
  • Crustacean and Mollusk Farming and Production
  • Fish Parasites and Diseases
  • Fish Farm Construction
  • Aquaculture Biotechnology
  • Aquatic Environment and Water Pollution
  • Fish Preservation and Processing
