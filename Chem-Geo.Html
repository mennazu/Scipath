🪨🧪 Geology & Chemistry

📌 About the Specialization

Geology and Chemistry is an interdisciplinary field that combines the study of Earth materials and geological processes with chemistry. It focuses on minerals, rocks, chemical analysis, geochemistry, environmental processes, and the chemical composition of Earth materials.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Earth Science and Chemistry
  • Analytical thinking
  • Basic laboratory skills
  • Observation skills
  • Interest in fieldwork

📚 Courses you will study

  • Structural Geology
  • Crystals and Minerals
  • Sedimentology
  • Igneous and Metamorphic Rocks
  • Geochemistry of Minerals
  • Petroleum Chemistry
  • Environmental Organic Chemistry
  • Scientific Analytical Methods
  • Instrumental Spectroscopic Analysis
  • Geochemical Exploration
  • Economic Geology
  • Hydrogeology and Well Design
  • Environmental Geology
  • GIS and Remote Sensing
