⚛️ Physics

📌 About the Specialization

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, forces, and the laws that govern the physical world. The specialization develops analytical thinking and problem-solving skills through theoretical and applied physics.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Physics
  • Strong Math Skills
  • Logical Thinking
  • Problem-Solving
  • Analytical Skills
  • attention to detail

  • Mathematical Physics
  • Optics

  • Solid State Physics
