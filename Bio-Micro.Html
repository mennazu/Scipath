🧬 Biochemistry & Microbiology

📌 About the Specialization

Biochemistry and Microbiology is an interdisciplinary specialization combining the study of biochemical processes, microorganisms, and molecular biology. It explores their applications in healthcare, biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, food safety, environmental protection, and scientific research.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Strong foundation in Chemistry and Biology
  • Interest in laboratory work
  • Attention to detail
  • Basic laboratory and analytical skills
  • Interest in microorganisms and biological processes

📚 Courses you will study

  • Biochemistry of Carbohydrates, Lipids, and Proteins
  • Microbial Analysis Techniques
  • Classification of Fungi, Yeasts, and Bacteria
  • Biochemistry of Nucleic Acids
  • Cell Biology and Tissue Culture
  • Carbohydrate and Lipid Metabolism
  • Microbial Physiology
  • Antibiotics
  • Medical Microbiology
  • Industrial Microbiology
  • Viruses and Immunology
  • Protein and Nucleic Acid Metabolism
  • Enzymes and Vitamins
  • Microbial Genetics and Biotechnology
  • Bioinformatics
  • Applied Microbiology
  • Clinical Biochemistry
  • Genetic Engineering
  • Quality Control
  • Biotechnology and Nanobiotechnology
