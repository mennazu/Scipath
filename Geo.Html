🪨🌍 Geology

📌 About the Specialization

Geology is the study of the Earth, its materials, structure, history, and the processes that shape it. The specialization combines mineralogy, rocks, fossils, geophysics, geochemistry, groundwater, petroleum, environmental geology, and field studies.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Earth sciences and geology
  • Basic knowledge of Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to read maps and analyze geological data
  • Willingness to do fieldwork

📚 Courses you will study

  • Mineralogy
  • Structural Geology
  • Petrology
  • Stratigraphy
  • Paleontology
  • Geophysics
  • Geochemistry
  • Petroleum Geology
  • Hydrogeology
  • Environmental Geology
  • GIS & Remote Sensing
  • Field Geology
