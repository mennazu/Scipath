🦠 Microbiology

📌 About the Specialization

Microbiology is the study of microorganisms such as bacteria, fungi, viruses, and algae. It explores their structure, genetics, physiology, and interactions with humans, animals, plants, and the environment, with applications in medicine, food, biotechnology, and industry.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Strong interest in Biology and microorganisms
  • Basic knowledge of Chemistry and Genetics
  • Laboratory and observation skills
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Interest in laboratory research
