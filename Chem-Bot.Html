🌱🧪 Botany & Chemistry

📌 About the Specialization

Plant and Chemistry is an interdisciplinary field that combines plant biology with chemistry. It focuses on plant structure, physiology, classification, ecology, and the chemical composition of plants, including natural products and plant-derived compounds.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Plants and Chemistry
  • Analytical thinking
  • Attention to detail
  • Basic laboratory skills
  • Interest in scientific research

📚 Courses you will study

  • Plant Physiology
  • Plant Taxonomy
  • Plant Ecology
  • Plant Tissue Culture
  • Plant Pathology
  • Plant Molecular Biology
  • Plant Biochemistry
  • Phytochemistry
  • Natural Products Chemistry
  • Organic Chemistry
  • Analytical Chemistry and Chromatography
  • Spectroscopic Analysis
  • Medicinal Chemistry and Pharmacognosy
  • Nucleic Acids and Molecular Biology
