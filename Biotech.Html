🧬🔬 Biotechnology

📌 About the Specialization

Biotechnology is a field that uses living organisms, cells, and biological processes to develop products and technologies for applications in medicine, agriculture, industry, and the environment.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Biology and Chemistry
  • Basic laboratory skills
  • Analytical thinking
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Interest in scientific research

  • Molecular Biology
  • Microbiology

  • Genetic Engineering
  • Microbial Biotechnology
  • Medical Biotechnology
  • Bioinformatics
  • Bioprocessing
  • Molecular Diagnostics and Gene Therapy

  • Food Biotechnology
  • Environmental Biotechnology
