🧪🏭 Applied Industrial Chemistry

📌 About the Specialization

A chemistry specialization focused on applying chemical principles to industrial processes, materials, manufacturing, and quality control across different industries.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Strong foundation in Chemistry
  • Interest in laboratory and industrial work
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Interest in chemical manufacturing and quality control

📚 Courses you will study

  • Aliphatic and Aromatic Chemistry
  • Physical Chemistry
  • Analytical Chemistry
  • Transition Metal Chemistry
  • Photochemistry
  • Electrochemistry
  • Kinetics and Catalysis
  • Electroanalytical and Chromatographic Methods
  • Petroleum Chemistry
  • Fertilizer Chemistry
  • Dyeing and Textile Printing Chemistry
  • Separation and Thermal Analysis
  • Polymers and Natural Products Chemistry
  • Chemical Manufacturing Processes
  • Pollutants Chemistry and Industrial Safety
  • Medicinal Chemistry and Drug Analysis
  • Cement and Building Materials Chemistry
  • Cosmetics and Perfume Chemistry
  • Petrochemical and Paints Chemistry
  • Nanochemistry

  • Steel and Metal Industries
  • Oil & Petroleum Industries
  • Fertilizer Industries
  • Food & Sugar Industries
  • Water & Electricity Companies
  • Soaps, Detergents & Oils Industries
  • Glass & Ceramics Industries
  • Chemical Analysis & Quality Control
  • Research Centers
