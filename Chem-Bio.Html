🧪🧬 Chemistry & Biochemistry

📌 About the Specialization

Chemistry and Biochemistry is an interdisciplinary field that combines the study of chemical substances and reactions with the chemical processes occurring in living organisms. It covers areas such as organic chemistry, biochemistry, metabolism, molecular biology, and biochemical analysis.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Chemistry and Biology
  • Analytical thinking
  • Attention to detail
  • Basic laboratory skills
  • Problem-solving skills

📚 Courses you will study

  • Organic Chemistry
  • Biochemistry
  • Carbohydrate and Lipid Metabolism
  • Bioenergetics
  • Protein and Nucleic Acid Metabolism
  • Enzymes and Vitamins
  • Medical Chemistry
  • Biochemistry of Microorganisms and Antibiotics
  • Biological Fluids and Functions
  • Biochemical Analysis and Chromatography
  • Molecular Biology
  • Nucleic Acids and Molecular Biology
  • Immunology
  • Cancer Biology
