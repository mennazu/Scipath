🌱 Botany

📌 About the Specialization

Botany is the study of plants, including their structure, growth, reproduction, physiology, classification, and interactions with the environment. It also covers plant biotechnology, ecology, and plant applications.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in plants and biology
  • Observation skills
  • Basic laboratory skills
  • Analytical thinking
  • Interest in field and laboratory work

📚 Courses you will study

  • Plant Physiology
  • Plant Anatomy
  • Plant Taxonomy
  • Plant Ecology
  • Plant Genetics
  • Plant Pathology
  • Plant Tissue Culture
  • Plant Molecular Biology
  • Plant Biotechnology
  • Plant Microbiology
  • Plant Biochemistry
  • Plant Systematics
  • Plant Diversity and Biodiversity
  • Medicinal Plants

🚀 Career Opportunites

  • Botanist
  • Plant Laboratory Specialist
  • Plant Research Assistant
  • Plant Biotechnology Specialist
  • Plant Pathology Specialist
  • Environmental Specialist
