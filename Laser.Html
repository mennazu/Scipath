🔦 Optics and laser sciences

📌 About the Specialization

A specialized program that focuses on optics and laser technologies and their applications in scientific research, industry, medicine, communications, and engineering. The program prepares students to work with optical and laser systems and modern photonics technologies.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Physics, Optics, and Technology
  • Good mathematical and analytical skills
  • Interest in working with optical and laser systems
