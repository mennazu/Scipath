🌳 Enviromental Science

📌 About the Specialization

Environmental Science is an interdisciplinary field that studies the relationship between living organisms and their environment. It combines biology, chemistry, geology, and environmental sciences to understand pollution, climate change, natural resources, and environmental problems.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in the environment and sustainability
  • Basic knowledge of Biology, Chemistry, and Geology
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Interest in environmental data and research
  • Willingness to do fieldwork
