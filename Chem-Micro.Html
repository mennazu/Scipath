🦠🧪 Microbiology & Chemistry

📌 About the Specialization

Microbiology and Chemistry is an interdisciplinary field that combines the study of microorganisms with chemistry. It focuses on microorganisms, their biological processes, chemical interactions, and applications in medicine, food, pharmaceuticals, environmental protection, and industry.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Microbiology and Chemistry
  • Basic laboratory skills
  • Analytical thinking
  • Attention to detail
  • Interest in scientific research
