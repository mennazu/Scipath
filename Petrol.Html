🛢️💧 Petroleum & Water Sciences

📌 About the Specialization

A specialized program that combines geology, petroleum sciences, and water sciences. During the first two years, students study basic geology along with supporting courses in chemistry, physics, mathematics, and university requirements. Specialized courses become more in-depth starting from the third year, covering petroleum exploration, oil-well development and processing, as well as groundwater assessment, exploration, and extraction.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Geology, Petroleum, and Water Sciences
  • Good understanding of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
  • Interest in fieldwork, exploration, and geological studies

  • Sedimentary Basin Analysis
  • Groundwater Quality Assessment
  • Hydrogeochemistry and Isotope Hydrology

  • Electrical and Electromagnetic Methods
  • Hydrogeophysical Water Exploration
  • Petroleum and Natural Gas Geology
  • Water Well Design
  • Reservoir Evaluation
  • Petroleum Exploration and Geophysical Data Processing
  • Water Well Drilling and Development
  • Petroleum Geochemistry
