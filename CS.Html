💻 Computer Science

📌 About the Specialization

Computer Science focuses on the study of computing, programming, algorithms, data structures, and software development. It helps students understand how computer systems work and how to design and develop software solutions to solve real-world problems.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Technology
  • logical Thinking
  • Problem-Solving Skills
  • Basic Mathematical Thinking
  • Patience and Attention to Detail
  • Willingness to learn Programming
