🐾🧪 Zoology & Chemistry

📌 About the Specialization

Animal Biology and Chemistry is an interdisciplinary field that combines the study of animals, their structure, physiology, genetics, and biological processes with chemistry. It also introduces chemical analysis and biochemical concepts used to understand biological systems and their applications.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in both Zoology and Chemistry
  • Good laboratory skills
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Attention to detail
  • Ability to work with biological and chemical samples
  • Interest in scientific research

📚 Courses you will study

  • Cell Biology & Genetics
  • Animal Physiology
  • Animal Ecology
  • Comparative Anatomy
  • Histology
  • Animal Behavior
  • Organic Chemistry
  • Physical Chemistry
  • Inorganic Chemistry
  • Natural Products Chemistry
  • Analytical Chemistry
  • Medical Instrumental Analysis
  • Environmental Organic Chemistry
  • Molecular Biology
