∑ Mathematics

📌 About the Specialization

Mathematics is a field that focuses on logical thinking, problem-solving, mathematical analysis, algebra, differential equations, statistics, mechanics, and mathematical modeling. It provides a strong foundation for scientific, technological, and analytical careers.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Logical Thinking
  • Strong Math Skills
  • Problem-Solving
  • Analytical Skills
  • Basic Computer Skills
  • Continuous Learning
  • Patience and attention to detail

  • Abstract Algebra
  • Real Analysis
