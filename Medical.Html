☢️🩻 Medical Radiation Physics

📌 About the Specialization

A specialized program that combines physics, radiation science, and medical applications. Students learn how radiation is used in medical diagnosis and treatment, radiation safety, imaging, and related technologies.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Physics and Radiation
  • Willingness to work with medical and technical applications
  • Interest in research, technology, and radiation safety

📚 Courses you will study

  • Medical Biophysics
  • Atomic Physics
  • Physics of Light
  • Medical Image Processing
  • Radiation Physics and Nuclear Reactors
  • Radiation Protection and Radiation Measurement
  • Ultrasound and MRI Physics
  • Laser and Its Medical Applications
  • Physics of Diagnostic and Therapeutic X-Ray Devices
  • Medical Electronics
  • Radiation Physics and Accelerators
  • Nuclear Medicine Physics
  • Biological Effects of Radiation
  • Radioisotopes and Their Medical Applications
  • Radiation Therapy Planning
  • Medical Imaging Physics
  • Nanotechnology and Its Medical Applications
  • Medical Materials Physics
