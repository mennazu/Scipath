🐞🧪 Entomology & Chemistry

📌 About the Specialization

Entomology & Chemistry combines the study of insects with chemistry. It explores insect biology, physiology, behavior, ecology, pest management, toxicology, and the chemical processes involved in insect control and environmental interactions.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in both Entomology and Chemistry
  • Good laboratory skills
  • Strong observation and analytical skills
  • Basic knowledge of Biology and Chemistry
  • Attention to detail
  • Interest in pest management and environmental applications

📚 Courses you will study

  • Insect Biology & Taxonomy
  • Insect Anatomy & Histology
  • Insect Physiology
  • Medical Entomology
  • Economic & Applied Entomology
  • Insect Pest Management
  • Insect Toxicology & Pesticides
  • Insect Ecology & Behavior
  • Organic Chemistry
  • Physical Chemistry
  • Natural Products Chemistry
  • Environmental Organic Chemistry
  • Instrumental Analysis
  • Surface Chemistry & Catalysis
