🧪 Chemistry

📌 About the Specialization

Chemistry focuses on the study of chemical substances, their composition, properties, and reactions. The specialization also focuses on practical and laboratory applications, helping students understand the behavior of different substances and apply chemical principles in fields such as pharmaceutical, food, environmental, and chemical industries.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Interest in Chemistry
  • Good Analytical Thinking
  • Attention to Detail
  • Problem-Solving Skills
  • Willingness to Work in Laboratories
  • Patience and Accuracy
