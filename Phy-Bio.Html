🔬 Physics & Biophysics

📌 Ap bout the Specialization

A specialization that combines physics with biology to understand biological systems and develop applications in healthcare, medical imaging, radiation therapy, and biomedical technology.

🧠 What do you need?

  • Strong background in Physics
  • Analytical thinking
  • Interest in Biology and Medical Sciences
  • Interest in Medical and Biomedical Applications

  • Solid State Physics

  • Laser Physics
